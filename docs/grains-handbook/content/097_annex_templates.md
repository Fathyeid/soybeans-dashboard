# ملحق 2: قوالب جاهزة للمراسلات والمستندات

القوالب التالية مكتوبة بالإنجليزية كما تُستخدم في السوق. عدّلها حسب العقد والظروف، وراجعها قانونيًا عند الحاجة. الأقواس المربعة [ ] تعني بيانات يجب ملؤها.

## م2.1 قالب عرض ملزم (Firm Offer)

?> Subject: FIRM OFFER – [Commodity] – [Quantity] – [Incoterm Port]
?> We are pleased to offer firm, subject to your acceptance by [time, time zone, date]:
?> Seller: [Legal name]
?> Buyer: [Legal name]
?> Commodity: [Commodity, origin, crop year]
?> Quality: [Full specifications – basis/max/min, analysis methods, allowances]
?> Quantity: [Quantity] MT +/- [x]% in [Seller's/Buyer's/Owner's] option at contract price
?> Price: USD [ ] PMT [Incoterm] [Port], [pricing mechanism if applicable]
?> Shipment/Delivery: [Period]
?> Load port / Discharge port: [ ]
?> Discharge/Load terms: [Rate] MT PWWD [SHEX/FHEX] [EIU/UU], NOR [conditions], demurrage USD [ ] / despatch [ ]
?> Payment: [Terms]
?> Inspection: [Surveyor], [final at loading/discharge], [costs]
?> Documents: [List]
?> Contract: [GAFTA/FOSFA No.], arbitration as per [Rules], English law
?> Brokerage: [ ]
?> Other terms: [ ]
?> Best regards, [Name, Title, Company]

## م2.2 قالب ملخص صفقة (Recap)

?> Subject: RECAP – Contract No. [ ] – [Commodity] – [Date]
?> We are pleased to confirm the following business concluded today [date] between:
?> Seller: [ ] / Buyer: [ ] / Broker: [ ]
?> [Repeat all terms as agreed, clause by clause]
?> Full contract to follow. Please confirm by return that the above correctly reflects our agreement.

## م2.3 قالب ترشيح سفينة في عقد FOB (Vessel Nomination)

?> Subject: VESSEL NOMINATION – Contract No. [ ] – [Quantity] [Commodity] FOB [Port]
?> Further to the above contract, we hereby nominate the following vessel:
?> Vessel name: [ ] / IMO: [ ] / Flag: [ ] / Built: [ ]
?> DWT: [ ] / LOA: [ ] m / Beam: [ ] m / Max draft: [ ] m
?> Holds/Hatches: [ ] / Gear: [ ] / Grain capacity: [ ] cbm
?> ETA load port: [date] / Laycan: [ ]
?> Quantity to load: [ ] MT +/- [ ]% MOLOO
?> Agents at load port: [Name, contact]
?> Owners/Managers: [ ] / P&I Club: [ ] / Class: [ ]
?> Please confirm acceptance of the vessel and terminal stem.

## م2.4 قالب إشعار التخصيص في عقد CIF/CFR (Notice of Appropriation)

?> Subject: NOTICE OF APPROPRIATION – Contract No. [ ]
?> We hereby appropriate the following shipment under the above contract:
?> Vessel: [ ] / B/L date: [ ] / B/L quantity: [ ] MT
?> Load port: [ ] / Discharge port: [ ] / ETA discharge port: [ ]
?> This notice is final. Shipping documents will follow as per contract.

## م2.5 قالب إشعار الاستعداد (Notice of Readiness)

?> NOTICE OF READINESS
?> To: [Shippers/Receivers/Charterers], c/o [Agents]
?> Vessel: [ ] / Voyage: [ ]
?> Please be advised that the above vessel arrived at [place] on [date] at [time LT] and is in all respects ready to [load/discharge] her cargo of [commodity] in accordance with the terms and conditions of the Charter Party dated [ ].
?> This Notice of Readiness is tendered at [time] on [date].
?> Master: _____________
?> Accepted at [time] on [date]: _____________ (Signature, without prejudice to the terms of the Charter Party)

## م2.6 قالب خطاب تعليمات للمعاين (Surveyor Instruction Letter)

?> Subject: INSTRUCTIONS – [Vessel] – [Commodity] – Contract No. [ ]
?> Dear Sirs, please attend on our behalf at [port] for the following:
?> 1. Hold inspection prior to loading; issue Certificate of Holds Cleanliness.
?> 2. Supervision of loading and continuous sampling as per GAFTA 124; samples sealed and distributed as follows: [ ].
?> 3. Analysis as per GAFTA 130 for: [list of parameters and methods].
?> 4. Initial and final draft surveys; issue Certificate of Weight.
?> 5. Supervision of fumigation; issue Fumigation Certificate.
?> 6. Certificates to be issued in [number] originals + [number] copies, worded as per attached L/C requirements.
?> 7. Daily reports by email to [ ].
?> 8. Drafts of all certificates to be sent for our approval before final issuance.
?> Your fees to be invoiced to [ ]. Please acknowledge receipt.

## م2.7 قالب خطاب تحفظ للربان عند التفريغ (Letter of Protest)

?> To: The Master of MV [ ] and/or Owners, c/o [Agents]
?> Date: [ ] / Port: [ ]
?> Dear Sir, we hereby hold you and your Owners responsible for [describe: wet/damaged/caked cargo found in hold No. X / shortage of X MT compared to B/L quantity / etc.] observed on [date/time] in the presence of [surveyors].
?> We reserve all our rights and those of our principals and insurers to claim for any loss and/or damage arising therefrom.
?> Please acknowledge receipt by signing below.
?> Yours faithfully, [Receivers/Agents]
?> Received (without prejudice): ______ Master

## م2.8 قالب إشعار مطالبة (Notice of Claim)

?> Subject: NOTICE OF CLAIM – Contract No. [ ] – [Vessel]
?> We hereby give you notice of our claim under the above contract in respect of [quality/condition/weight/demurrage/default] as follows: [brief description].
?> Our provisional claim amounts to USD [ ], subject to final assessment.
?> Supporting documents: [list].
?> This notice is given without prejudice to any other rights we may have under the contract, and we reserve our right to commence arbitration in accordance with [GAFTA/FOSFA] Rules within the applicable time limits.

## م2.9 قالب إشعار تعيين محكّم (Notice of Appointment of Arbitrator)

?> Subject: ARBITRATION – Contract No. [ ] dated [ ]
?> Disputes have arisen under the above contract. In accordance with the [GAFTA Arbitration Rules No. 125 / FOSFA Rules], we hereby appoint [name] as our arbitrator.
?> We call upon you to appoint your arbitrator within the time limit provided in the Rules, failing which we shall apply to the Association for an appointment on your behalf.

## م2.10 قالب طلب تمديد شحن (Extension Notice)

?> Subject: CLAIM FOR EXTENSION OF SHIPMENT PERIOD – Contract No. [ ]
?> In accordance with the extension clause of the above contract, we hereby claim an extension of the shipment period by [up to X] days. The price adjustment will be applied as per the contract terms based on the actual B/L date.

## م2.11 قالب تأكيد تسوية نقدية (Washout Confirmation)

?> Subject: WASHOUT CONFIRMATION – Contract No. [ ]
?> We confirm that the above contract for [quantity] MT [commodity] at USD [ ] PMT is mutually washed out at a settlement price of USD [ ] PMT. The difference of USD [ ] PMT × [quantity] = USD [ ] shall be paid by [party] to [party] within [ ] banking days. Upon payment, both parties are released from all obligations under the said contract.

## م2.12 قالب خطاب ضمان للتسليم دون بوليصة أصلية (مختصر توضيحي)

?> To: [Owners] of MV [ ]
?> Description of goods: [ ] / Quantity: [ ] / Load port: [ ] / Discharge port: [ ] / B/L No.: [ ] / Shipper: [ ] / Consignee: [ ]
?> The above goods were shipped on the above vessel by [ ] and consigned to [ ] for delivery at [port], but the bills of lading have not arrived and we hereby request you to deliver the said goods to [ ] without production of the original bills of lading.
?> In consideration of your complying with our above request, we hereby agree as follows: [indemnity, defence costs, security for arrest, delivery up of original B/Ls when received, liability joint and several, law and jurisdiction]...
?> (يُستخدم النص الكامل للنموذج المعياري الصادر عن المجموعة الدولية لأندية P&I)
