# ملحق 2أ: نماذج شروط خاصة كاملة لعقود شراء وبيع

النصوص التالية نماذج للشروط الخاصة (Special Conditions) التي تُكتب في صفحة العقد قبل الإحالة للعقد النموذجي. هي نماذج تعليمية يجب مراجعتها قانونيًا وتكييفها لكل صفقة.

## م2أ.1 عقد شراء قمح FOB (بنمط GAFTA 49)

?> PURCHASE CONTRACT No. [ ] – Date: [ ]
?> Buyers: [ ] / Sellers: [ ]
?> 1. Goods: [Origin] Milling Wheat, crop [year], in bulk.
?> 2. Quality: Protein min 12.5% (on dry matter basis), Moisture max 13.5%, Test weight min 78 kg/hl, Falling number min 250 sec, Wet gluten min 23%, Foreign matter max 2% (of which max 0.5% inorganic), Broken kernels max 4%, Bug-damaged max 1%, Ergot max 0.05%, Free from live insects, sound and merchantable, free from abnormal odour.
?> 3. Quantity: [ ] MT, 10% more or less in Buyers' option at contract price, to be declared with final vessel nomination.
?> 4. Price: USD [ ] per MT FOB stowed and trimmed, one safe berth [port].
?> 5. Delivery period: Vessel to present between [ ] and [ ], both days inclusive.
?> 6. Nomination: Buyers to give preliminary notice of vessel's ETA at least [15] days before ETA, and final nomination with vessel's particulars at least [7] days before ETA. Buyers have the right to substitute the vessel provided the substitute's ETA is within the original delivery period.
?> 7. Loading: At the rate of [ ] MT per WWD SHINC, NOR to be tendered any time day or night, laytime to commence [12] hours after valid NOR. Demurrage/despatch USD [ ] / half demurrage per day pro rata. Sellers responsible for terminal stem and loading.
?> 8. Carrying charges: If the vessel is not presented within the delivery period, Buyers may, by notice before the last day of the delivery period, extend the period by up to [21] days against payment of carrying charges of USD [ ] per MT per [ ] days.
?> 9. Weight and quality: Final at loading as per certificates of [independent superintendent], appointed and paid by Sellers. Buyers may attend at their cost. Sampling as per GAFTA 124, analysis as per GAFTA 130.
?> 10. Payment: 100% net cash against presentation of documents, by telegraphic transfer within [3] banking days of presentation of: commercial invoice, full set of B/Ls, certificates of weight and quality, phytosanitary certificate, certificate of origin, fumigation certificate [and others].
?> 11. Sanctions: [Standard sanctions clause].
?> 12. Other terms: As per GAFTA Contract No. 49 (latest edition), arbitration as per GAFTA Arbitration Rules No. 125, English law.

## م2أ.2 عقد بيع قمح CFR مرآة (بنمط GAFTA 48)

?> SALES CONTRACT No. [ ] – Date: [ ]
?> Sellers: [ ] / Buyers: [ ]
?> 1. Goods: As per purchase contract (same description and crop).
?> 2. Quality: Identical to purchase contract specifications [plus any destination requirements agreed and achievable].
?> 3. Quantity: [ ] MT, 10% more or less in Owners' option at contract price.
?> 4. Price: USD [ ] per MT CFR FO one safe berth [discharge port].
?> 5. Shipment: B/L dated between [ ] and [ ] (shipment period ending at least 5 days after the purchase delivery period).
?> 6. Notice of appropriation: Within [7] days after B/L date.
?> 7. Discharge: At the rate of [ ] MT per WWD [FHEX/SHEX] [UU/EIU], NOR WIBON WIPON WIFPON WCCON, laytime commencing [12] hours after NOR, demurrage USD [ ] (not less than C/P demurrage) / despatch half demurrage on laytime saved. Time lost waiting for berth to count as laytime.
?> 8. Weight and quality: Final at loading as per certificates of [same superintendent as purchase contract].
?> 9. Payment: Irrevocable L/C at sight, confirmed by a first-class bank acceptable to Sellers, opened not later than [ ] days before the first day of shipment, valid [ ] days, in accordance with the L/C format attached as Annex 1.
?> 10. Documents: [Same as purchase + any additional documents obtainable].
?> 11. LOI: If original documents are not available on vessel's arrival, Sellers to provide LOI as per Annex 2 against which Buyers shall pay.
?> 12. Other terms: As per GAFTA Contract No. 48 (latest edition), arbitration as per GAFTA 125, English law.
